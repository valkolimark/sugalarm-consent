Sugalarm is a household continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) and alerting service operated by Seecose, LLC (“Sugalarm,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Sugalarm or by agreeing to receive our text messages, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information — name, email address, and login credentials for account owners and household members.
  • Contact information — the mobile phone numbers of emergency contacts you add, and the verification status of those contacts.
  • Alert preferences — thresholds, quiet-hours settings, reminders, and which contacts receive which alerts.

Health information

Because Sugalarm exists to monitor blood glucose, we process glucose readings and related CGM data for the household members you choose to monitor. We treat this as sensitive information and use it only to provide the monitoring and alerting service.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and kiosk data — device identifiers, connection status, app and agent version, and basic diagnostic logs.
  • Usage data — how the app and kiosk are used, for reliability and troubleshooting.

How we use your information

  • To deliver glucose monitoring, generate alerts, and send the text messages you or a contact have consented to.
  • To verify emergency contacts before any alerts are sent to them.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the service.
  • To respond to support requests and to comply with legal obligations.

SMS / text messaging and your consent

We send transactional text messages — glucose alerts, a one-time confirmation when a contact is added, and optional test messages. How consent is collected is described on our SMS Alerts & Consent page.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number, mobile opt-in information, or SMS consent with any third parties for their own marketing purposes. Phone numbers and SMS consent collected by Sugalarm are not shared with third parties or affiliates for promotional use under any circumstances.

A contact may opt out at any time by replying STOP, and may reply HELP for assistance.

How we share information

We share information only as needed to run the service:

  • Within your household — account owners and members can see the data and alerts relevant to the people they monitor.
  • Service providers — we use vetted vendors strictly to deliver the service, such as our SMS delivery provider, CGM data sources, and cloud hosting. They may process information only on our behalf and under contract, not for their own purposes.
  • Legal and safety — when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the public.
  • Business transfers — if Seecose, LLC is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may transfer subject to this Policy.

As stated above, sharing for third-party marketing never occurs, and SMS opt-in data is never part of any of the transfers described here for marketing purposes.

Data retention

We keep information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service, then delete or de-identify it within a reasonable period, except where longer retention is required by law. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s privacy

Sugalarm accounts are created and managed by adults. A household may monitor the glucose of a minor, but that data is provided and controlled by the responsible adult account owner. We do not knowingly allow children to create their own accounts or to receive marketing of any kind. If you believe a child has provided us information improperly, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

  • Access, correct, or update your account information in the app.
  • Opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP.
  • Request a copy or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws. We honor valid requests as required by law.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Continued use after changes take effect means you accept the updated Policy.
