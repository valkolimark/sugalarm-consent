Early Access Program

Help us build calmer nights for the people watching over someone they love.

Sugalarm watches a household member’s continuous glucose monitor (CGM) and alerts the whole household — on the phone, the bedside kiosk, the watch, and trusted emergency contacts — the moment blood sugar goes low or high. You’re invited to try it before everyone else and shape what it becomes.

What you’re joining

Early Access is a small group of real households using Sugalarm day-to-day. You’ll get the app early, your feedback goes straight to the team, and the things you report get fixed fast. It’s free during testing.

What we’ll ask of you

  • 1

    Use it like it’s yours. Connect your Dexcom or Libre, set your thresholds, and let Sugalarm run on your phone or a bedside kiosk for a few weeks.

  • 2

    Try the alarms safely. Use the built-in Test alarms in Settings to hear how lows, highs, and urgent alerts behave — no need to wait for a real event.

  • 3

    Tell us what’s working and what isn’t. Bugs, confusing screens, missed or late alerts, feature ideas — every note is read by the team via the in-app feedback form.

Request your invite

Tell us a bit about you and which device you’ll test on. We’ll email you an invite with everything you need to get started.

What happens next

  • →

    You’ll get an invite by email. iPhone testers receive a TestFlight invite; Android testers receive a link to install. We send these to the address you provide above.

  • →

    Install & sign in. Create your account, connect your CGM, and you’re live — usually in a few minutes, but it could take up to 24 hours.

One important note on safety

Sugalarm is a secondary display — not a medical device. Keep your CGM’s own alarms turned on, and always confirm with a fingerstick if a reading and how you feel don’t match. Sugalarm helps the household stay aware; it doesn’t replace your CGM or your care team’s guidance.
