Sugalarm is a home glucose-monitoring app that watches a household member’s continuous glucose monitor (CGM) and notifies their trusted emergency contacts by SMS when blood sugar falls into a low or urgent low range. This page describes our text-message program, how a person consents to receive these alerts, and how to stop them.

The messaging program

What texts Sugalarm sends

Sugalarm sends transactional health alerts only — no marketing, no promotions. A contact receives a text when the monitored person’s glucose crosses a low or urgent-low threshold, plus a one-time confirmation text when they are first added and an optional test message the account owner can trigger to confirm delivery.

Urgent low alert Sugalarm URGENT LOW: [name] is [54] mg/dL as of [11:18 AM]. Please check on them now. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.
Low alert Sugalarm LOW alert: [name] is [68] mg/dL as of [11:18 AM].
Confirmation (sent once, at opt-in) Sugalarm: [owner] added you as an emergency glucose-alert contact. Reply YES to confirm, STOP to opt out. Msg & data rates may apply.
Consent

How you opt in

No one receives a Sugalarm text without first confirming it themselves. Consent is captured per contact, in the recipient’s own words, before any alert is ever sent:

  1. A Sugalarm account owner enters a trusted contact’s mobile number in the app under Settings → Alerts → Emergency contacts.
  2. Sugalarm sends that number a one-time confirmation text asking the person to reply YES to agree to receive glucose alerts.
  3. Only after the contact replies YES are they marked verified and begin receiving alerts. Numbers that never confirm are never texted again.
  4. A verified contact can reply STOP at any time to opt out immediately.
Your options

Frequency, rates, opt-out & help

Message frequency

Varies. Alerts are event-driven and only sent when glucose crosses a low threshold, so volume depends on the monitored person’s readings.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Sugalarm does not charge for texts; your mobile carrier’s standard rates apply.

Opt out

Reply STOP to any message to stop all texts. You can also ask the account owner to remove you in the app.

Get help

Reply HELP, or contact us at [support@sugalarm.com].

Privacy

What we do with your number

Your mobile number is used solely to deliver the glucose alerts described above for the household that added you.

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile opt-in information or SMS consent with any third parties for their own marketing or any other purpose. Phone numbers collected for SMS are not shared with third parties or affiliates for promotional use.
